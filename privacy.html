Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information FutureCost may collect and how it is used.

1. Information you provide

If account features are enabled, FutureCost may collect information you submit directly, such as your name, email address, and login details.

2. Calculation and saved decision data

FutureCost currently stores certain saved calculator history in your browser using local storage so your saved decisions can persist on your device.

3. Analytics and technical data

FutureCost may collect technical information such as device type, browser, approximate location, usage patterns, and page interactions to improve the service.

4. How information is used

  • To operate and improve the site
  • To provide account-related features
  • To maintain security and prevent abuse
  • To communicate with users when necessary

5. Sharing

FutureCost does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers who help operate the product, subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. Data retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for product, security, legal, or operational purposes.

7. Your choices

You can clear browser-stored history by clearing local site data in your browser. If account systems are added, account deletion and data requests may be supported later.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: privacy@futurecost.net
